Society Maintenance Policy

Monthly maintenance for each flat is ₹4,000.

Maintenance must be paid before the 10th of every month.

Residents who fail to pay maintenance before the 10th are charged a late fee of ₹200.

Residents can pay maintenance using UPI, bank transfer, or other payment methods approved by the society.

Swimming pool timings are 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM.

The society office is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Visitors must be registered with the society security desk.

Residents should contact the society office for maintenance-related complaints.
